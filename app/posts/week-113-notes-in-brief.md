Early and brief weeknotes from me, as I’m away from tomorrow for a 5 day interrail trip around Europe, for which the only thing I’ve booked is the outward and return Eurostars. The rest is open to spontaneity.

This week I’ve:

* run the [NHS prototype kit training course](https://prototype-kit.service-manual.nhs.uk/training-course/) for a new group of 9 designers, who all seemed to enjoy it and have sent some nice feedback
* been finding and collating examples of all the different NHS tests, checks and screening you can get via the NHS App and website
* drafted a rationale for why I think a cohesive ‘Get a test’ journey in the NHS App makes sense, and how it might be developed incrementally
* attended a playback from a team looking at how the NHS App might be able to notify users about more things than just messages
* had a chat with a service designer who’s joined recently and is exploring a strategy for giving parents more visibility and control of their children’s health in the NHS App
* spoken to [Giles](https://gilest.org/index.html) and co about next steps for managing the [NHS digital prevention services website](https://www.digital-prevention-services.nhs.uk)
* completed some admin tasks to enable our GitHub repos to remain public
* nudged a couple more things along in the emerging [NHS App iOS design system](https://design-system.nhsapp.service.nhs.uk/ios/) and didn’t fall into too many SwiftUI rabbitholes
* started making plans for NHS Design History Day 2026 - hoping to make it even bigger and better than [last year](/posts/week-77-design-history-day/)

That’ll do. See you next week!
