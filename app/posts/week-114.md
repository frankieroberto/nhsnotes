This time last week I was in San Sebastián, in T-shirt weather, where I paddled in the warm ocean and stayed out late pintxos-hopping the bars. On Tuesday I was back at work in London, where it was rainy, cold and grey. Consequently I was tired and a bit grumpy this week. My mood wasn’t helped by being blocked on a few work fronts, and with nothing to immediately focus on, my attention was scattered and I ended the week feeling unproductive. That’s ok. This work has highs and lows. Next week will be better.
